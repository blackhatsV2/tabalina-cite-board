# Later

Good ideas that are not in the MVP.

Parking something here is a decision, not a loss. It is how the MVP
stays small enough to actually ship by Session 3.

## Parked from the CITE Week interview

- **Announcement tab** — broadcast messages to all users. Nice for "CITE Week starts Monday," but not needed for the task approval flow. Add once the MVP is live.
- **Separate OIC dashboard** — the OIC needs a place to create and assign tasks. If the MVP is built around the faculty adviser's view, this becomes the second phase.
- **Separate dean dashboard** — the dean needs to approve tasks. If the MVP is built around the faculty adviser's view, this becomes the third phase.
- **Task creation/edit by faculty** — only the OIC assigns tasks. Faculty confirm, comment, or suggest changes.
- **File attachments or multimedia** — keep it text-only for the MVP.
- **Calendar sync or notifications** — in-app status is enough for the MVP. No email or SMS yet.
- **User accounts & login (Supabase Auth)** — Email/password login for faculty advisers, OIC, and Dean. Planned right after the core commenting MVP loop is complete.
